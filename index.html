About REMBRLS

REM Braille Services LLC is a professional Accessibility & Document-Production Services company specializing in high-accuracy Braille transcription, formatting, embossing, and tactile graphics. We support schools, publishers, agencies, and organizations that require reliable, standards-compliant accessible materials.

Our Mission

To deliver precise, readable, and instructionally sound Braille materials that empower learners, support accessibility, and uphold national Braille standards.

Our Expertise

  • Unified English Braille (UEB)
  • UEB Guidelines for Technical Material (UEB-GT)
  • Nemeth Code for Mathematics & Science Notation
  • Tactile graphics following BANA/APH guidelines
  • Educational material production for K–12 and adult programs

Services

REM Braille Services LLC provides end-to-end Braille and accessible document-production services tailored to educational, institutional, and professional environments.

Braille Transcription

Converting print text to accurate, properly formatted Braille using UEB, UEB-GT, Nemeth, and other required standards.

Braille Embossing

Producing high-quality embossed Braille documents, books, manuals, and materials for readers of all ages.

Document Formatting

Structuring documents to meet Braille layout standards, including headings, tables, lists, graphics, and technical notation.

Proofreading & Quality Assurance

Ensuring accuracy, consistency, and compliance with Braille rules through detailed review and quality checks.

Tactile Graphics Production

Creating raised-line diagrams, maps, charts, graphs, and STEM illustrations designed for tactile readability.

Educational Material Preparation

Producing textbooks, worksheets, tests, and learning aids in Braille for K–12 and adult education programs.

Custom Braille Projects

Labels, cards, menus, manuals, and specialty Braille items tailored to your organization’s accessibility needs.
