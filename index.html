About REMBRLS

REM Braille Services LLC is a professional Accessibility & Document-Production Services company specializing in high-accuracy Braille transcription, formatting, embossing, and tactile graphics. We support schools, publishers, agencies, and organizations that require reliable, standards-compliant accessible materials.

Our Mission

To deliver precise, readable, and instructionally sound Braille materials that empower learners, support accessibility, and uphold national Braille standards.

Our Expertise

  • Unified English Braille (UEB)
  • UEB Guidelines for Technical Material (UEB-GT)
  • Nemeth Code for Mathematics & Science Notation
  • Tactile graphics following BANA/APH guidelines
  • Educational material production for K–12 and adult programs
