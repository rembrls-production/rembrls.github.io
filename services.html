Our Services

REM Braille Services LLC provides high‑quality, standards‑compliant Braille and tactile production for schools, publishers, accessibility coordinators, and organizations nationwide. All materials are produced using Unified English Braille (UEB), Nemeth Code, and current tactile graphic guidelines to ensure clarity, accuracy, and full accessibility.

Braille Transcription

Professional transcription of print materials into fully accessible Braille using UEB conventions. We prepare textbooks, worksheets, tests, manuals, and custom documents with precise formatting, correct structure, and publisher‑ready layout.

Tactile Graphics

Clear, readable tactile graphics designed according to BANA and APH guidelines. We produce raised‑line diagrams, maps, charts, graphs, geometric figures, and STEM illustrations that convey essential information through touch.

Embossed Braille Production

High‑quality embossed Braille output for textbooks, instructional materials, tests, and custom projects. All embossed volumes are formatted for easy navigation, durability, and classroom use.

Nemeth Code

Accurate transcription of mathematics and science content using The Nemeth Braille Code for Mathematics and Science Notation 2022. Ideal for districts and states requiring Nemeth for STEM subjects.

Science and Math Braille (UEB Technical)

UEB Technical formatting for mathematics, chemistry, physics, and advanced STEM content. We follow the Unified English Braille Guidelines for Technical Material (UEB‑GT) to ensure clarity, consistency, and full accessibility.

Custom Accessibility Projects

Specialized Braille and tactile solutions tailored to your organization’s needs. This includes custom diagrams, specialty documents, labels, manuals, training materials, and unique accessibility requests.
